For this project, we designed a plushy with three LEDs in a parallel circuit with a button and an on/off switch that both affect all LEDs. I made my plushy into a creature with a button up shirt on, with the LEDs being the buttons. You hold their hand to push the button for the lights to turn on!

Here is the front of my completed plushy: 
![Completed Plushy front](https://ferguson628.github.io/assets/img/CPfront.jpeg) 

And here is the back: 
![Completed Plushy back](https://ferguson628.github.io/assets/img/CPback.jpeg) 

My biggest tip would be to not underestimate the time that all the additional details take. I finished the circuit stitching, then since I had the "main part" finished, I felt I was close to done. But with all the additional stitching it still took another couple hours to finish the rest of the project.
