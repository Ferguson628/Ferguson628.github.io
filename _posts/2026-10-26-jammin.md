My biggest tip to my past self would be to test the light sensor with my phone flashlight, rather than my desk lamp. I'm still not sure why, but the sensor would very inconsistently read the desk lamp correctly, even when it was held very close to the sensor. My light values would jump every couple seconds from values between 0 and 4 (most often 0) around half the time, to values in the 900s the other half. However, when I used my phone flashlight, the sensor was able to read it perfectly. Still not sure why that was the case. But using the flashlight from the start would've saved me some time trying to figure out if there was something wrong with my code/sensor.
