For this assignment, we were instructed to select a song and find or write Arduino code to play that song when a button is pressed. I selected *Für Elise* in A Minor by Ludwig van Beethoven. I mostly selected this song for two reasons: 1) I remembered learning it on piano as a kid and I know the beginning of it well enough that I'd be able to tell when I messed something up, and 2) it is in the public domain, so I could find free sheet music for it on [Musecore] (https://musescore.com/classicman/fur-elise?srsltid=AYCX9vJ-Oq018CYNJhA_y0eU9UfRMDoi40QtYsMUttTse4_J788vAb1u).
