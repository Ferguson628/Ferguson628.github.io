For this assignment, we had to write code that buzzes our name in Morse code on a vibe board when a switch is off, and buzzes SOS when the switch is on.

Here is a photo of my vibe set-up:
![Help! Vibe & switch set-up](https://ferguson628.github.io/assets/img/jammin.jpeg) 

I had fun writing everything in my code and *then* testing all of it at once, and, though I wouldn't recommend that strategy in the future, the only thing I had switched up was the meanings of LOW/HIGH on the switch. Funnily enough, I had misremembered when our name was supposed to buzz (switch off) and when SOS was (switch on), so after changing it, I had to switch it back after reading over the assignment one more time before submission.
