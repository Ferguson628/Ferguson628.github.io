## Piece #1: Omnidroid
My first final project piece will be a 3D Omnidroid (from *The Incredibles* (2005)) with a button and two LEDs. When it is turned on, the red LED is lit, and while the button is pressed, blue LED also turns on. This is the first of three movie robots I will make.

### Materials list
- 1 styrofoam ball, 4 in. diameter
- 2 pieces of black felt
- 1 piece of red fabric, 1 in. x 4 in.
- Some cardboard
- Hot glue
- Conductive thread
- 1 switched battery holder
- 1 coin cell battery
- 1 red LED
- 1 blue LED
- ~21 black pipe cleaners
- Black acrylic paint
- Black Sharpie marker
- (Pins – used in the process, though not the final product)

### Tips to my past self
By biggest tip to my past self would be to consider that pipe cleaners are made of wire! So they are conductive! I only considered this and the problems that might cause after having made the piece with the blue LED coming out of the head from pipe cleaner and hot glued it inside the head. My second tip is that though it does work as insulation, covering a pipe cleaner in hot glue is maybe not the most attractive solution.
