For this assignment, we had to write code to turn various Ardunio LEDs on depending on the number of times the code had looped. When the number of loops (n) reached a multiple of 5, LED 5 would turn on. At a multiples of 6, 7, or 8, LEDs 6, 7, or 8 would turn on, respectively. A light would stay on until n reached a multiple of a different element of {5, 6, 7, 8}. If a number is a multiple of more than one of 5, 6, 7, & 8, the only the lowest LED number turns on.
