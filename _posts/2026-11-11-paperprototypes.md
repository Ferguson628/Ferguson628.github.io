## Movie Robots
I will be making various robots from some of my favorite movies!

### R2-D2
My first final project piece will be an R2-D2 shirt patch (from *Star Wars* (1977)) with a switch, light sensor, RBG LED, and buzzer. When it gets dark, his LED flashes red and blue and he screams.

### WALL-E
My second piece will be WALL·E (from *WALL·E* (2008)), sewn onto a pair of shorts, with a homemade switch built into his hand. When his hand is held, the LEDs light up in a heart above his head. Since there are 8 LEDs, there will be two circuits each with their own battery.


![WALL-E Paper Prototype](https://ferguson628.github.io/assets/img/wallepaper.jpeg) 

### Omnidroid
My final project piece will be a 3D Omnidroid (from *The Incredibles* (2005)) with a button and two LEDs. When turned on, the red LED turns on, and while the button is pressed, blue LED also turns on.

![Omnidroid Paper Prototype](https://ferguson628.github.io/assets/img/omnidroidpaper.jpeg)
